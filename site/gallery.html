The work

Two pieces, two materials.

Every project starts as a different kind of block. Here's what came out of the last two.

Motorcycle dealership

CJP Motorcycles

CJP needed their entire floor inventory browsable online, plus a way for staff — not developers — to keep it current. We built a directory-style listing site backed by an admin dashboard for full catalogue management.

Sales staff can add a new bike, update pricing, mark something sold, and swap photos without touching code. The public-facing directory updates instantly, with filtering by make, model, year, and price.

Music production studio

DHR Productions

DHR wanted a site that sounded like their work looked — built around reels, sessions, and a straightforward way for artists to reach out. We designed and built a studio site centered on media, with a booking-inquiry form that routes straight to the team.

The result puts the audio and video work front and center rather than burying it under agency copy, with a clean portfolio structure that's easy to add new sessions to over time.
